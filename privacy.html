Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Campus Menace (“we”, “us”) is a free browser game available at campus-menace.vercel.app. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at support@campus-menace.sushantaryal.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address: to create your account, sign you in, verify your email and reset your password.
  • Username: the name you choose, which is shown on the leaderboard.
  • Password: stored only as a secure one-way hash by our authentication provider. We never see or store your actual password.

Information created when you play

  • Run results: for each run, your score, distance, takedowns, close calls, how long it lasted, how it ended, the level seed, start and finish times, and whether the result passed our fair-play checks.
  • Account activity: when your account was created, when your email was verified and when you last signed in.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting and database providers receive your IP address, browser type and request times when your device connects to them. They use this to deliver the service and protect it from abuse, and keep it in short-lived logs.

Information stored on your device

The game stores a few things in your browser’s local storage: your sign-in session (so you stay signed in) and your settings (sound, graphics quality, steering mode and tilt sensitivity). This is needed for the game to work and is never used to track you. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To send emails you need: email verification and password resets. We don’t send marketing emails.
  • To save your runs and show your best scores, rank and history.
  • To show the leaderboard and keep it fair, including rejecting results that look tampered with.
  • To keep the game secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on these legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the game and your account when you sign up) and our legitimate interests (security, preventing cheating and abuse, and improving the game).

4. What other players can see

Your username and your best scores appear on the leaderboard, which signed-in players can see. Your email address, your password and your individual run history are never shown to other players.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers who process data only to run the game for us:

6. Where your data is stored

Our providers store and process data in the United States. If you play from outside the US, your information will be transferred there. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses offered by our providers.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and runs for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your email, username, password hash and every run are deleted from our database straight away and you’re removed from the leaderboard. Copies may remain for a short time in our providers’ encrypted backups and logs until they are overwritten in their normal cycle.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: open Profile → Download my data to get a copy of everything we store about you.
  • Delete your account: open Profile → Delete my account. This is permanent.
  • Change your password: from your profile, or with “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.
  • Correct or ask about your data: email us. Usernames can’t be changed in the game, but we can help if yours needs fixing.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK, California or other US states with privacy laws), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. Email us to make a request; we’ll respond within the time the law requires and may need to confirm the request comes from your account’s email. If you’re in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Campus Menace is not meant for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please contact us.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and database rules that let each player read only their own private data. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use anywhere else.

11. Do Not Track

We don’t track you across other websites, and no third parties track you on ours. Because we don’t do this kind of tracking, the game works the same whether or not your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We’ll change the date at the top, and if a change is significant we’ll let you know in the game before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@campus-menace.sushantaryal.com
